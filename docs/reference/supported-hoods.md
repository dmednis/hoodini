# Supported hoods

| Hood / model | Profile | Evidence | Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| Elica Hidden Advance (PRF0182867A) | HIDDEN_ADVANCE | **Tested** | Fan speeds, boost, light and diagnostic capabilities |
| Other Elica Connect models | Generic fallback | **Unverified** on other hardware | Observed capabilities only; no boost |

The generic profile is inferred from the protocol and does not mean a model has been tested. For a new hood, [report your model](https://github.com/dmednis/hoodini/issues/new?template=compatibility.yml) with manually reviewed, redacted observations; do not attach identifiers or credentials. See [adding a hood model](../contributing/adding-a-hood-model.md) and [known limitations](known-limitations.md).
